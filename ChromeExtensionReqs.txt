~~~Purpose~~~
The Chrome Extension will encourage users to attempt tasks independently before receiving AI assistance.
-
The extension is intended to:
Promote confidence
Encourage autonomy
Strengthen problem-solving
Increase awareness of AI dependence
-
The extension will not block users from accessing AI.
Instead, it will introduce a short reflection activity before AI assistance is provided.


~~~Problem being Solved~~~
Many users immediately rely on AI tools before attempting to solve problems independently.
-
As a result:
Confidence may decrease
Critical thinking may decrease
Problem-solving skills may weaken
-
The extension aims to encourage users to attempt solutions first while preserving access to AI assistance afterward.


~~~Target Users~~~
Primary Users:
Winston-Salem State University students majoring in Computer Science,
Information Technology, or any other related majors


~~~Core Features~~~
Feature 1: AI Detection
Purpose:
Detect when a participant visits an AI platform.

Initial Targets:
ChatGPT
Microsoft Copilot
Gemini
-
Feature 2: Intervention Prompt
Purpose
Require an independent attempt before assistance.

Prompt Example:
Before using AI assistance, briefly explain how you would approach this problem.

Requirements:
Text box
Submit button
Continue button
-
Feature 3: Data Logging
Purpose:
Collect research data.

The extension should record:
Participant ID
Timestamp
Session Number
Attempt Submitted
Time Before AI Access
-
Feature 4: Export Data
Purpose:
Allow research data to be exported.

Format:
CSV


~~~Data Not Collected~~~
The extension will NOT collect:
Passwords
Names
Emails
Financial Data
Academic Records
Messages
Browser History
AI Prompts
AI Responses


~~~User Workflow~~~
Participant Opens ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or Gemini
        ↓
Extension Detects Site
        ↓
Intervention Prompt Appears
        ↓
Participant Writes Attempt
        ↓
Participant Clicks Submit
        ↓
Data Logged
        ↓
AI Access Granted


~~~Success Criteria~~~
Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The extension can:
✅ Load in Chrome

✅ Detect ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or Gemini

✅ Display prompt

✅ Accept response

✅ Log timestamp

✅ Export CSV
